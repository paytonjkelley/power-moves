Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Love.

More than a business. A commitment to connection, community, and doing good in the moments that matter.

Power Moves is a Texas-founded company providing portable power bank rentals and curated local experiences, built by two founders who believe technology should help people stay present, not pull them away.

How It Started

A simple service with a bigger purpose.

“Even the simplest service can make a difference.”

Some of life’s best moments get cut short by a dying phone.

A concert with friends. A dinner that runs late. A long day of meetings across town. Too often, staying connected has meant stepping away from the moment to stand next to an outlet.

Power Moves began with two founders and a shared vision to change that. We believed charging should move with people instead of holding them in place, and that something as simple as a portable power bank could open the door to something bigger.

So we built a company around connection in every sense: keeping devices powered, helping people discover what’s happening in their city, supporting the local businesses that make a community feel like home, and treating every person we serve with care.

We’re guided by faith and grounded in love. That shapes how we build, how we partner, and how we show up for the people around us.

The Verse That Guides Us

“Let all that you do be done in love.”

1 CORINTHIANS 16:14

This verse is the standard we hold ourselves to. Whether we’re helping a customer find a charger, partnering with a local venue, or hiring our team, we want every interaction to reflect kindness, integrity, and genuine care.

From time to time you may find a short verse or a word of encouragement on our stations or website. It’s there for anyone who wants it, and never required.
